# Beginso template catalogue

77 templates in 13 categories, built in three waves. Wave 1 (25) is written in `wave1Templates.ts`. Waves 2 and 3 follow the same recipe, and each wave is reviewed before the next.

Looks: CC = Clean Corporate, WF = Warm Friendly, ES = Elegant Serif, BE = Bold Event, PB = Playful Bright, CA = Calm Care, MP = Minimal Paper, DT = Dark Tech, ED = Editorial, AP = Appetite.

## Categories (sidebar order)
1. Leads & Contact (10)
2. Orders & Payments (5)
3. Events & RSVPs (10)
4. Bookings (7)
5. Feedback & Surveys (8)
6. HR & Hiring (4)
7. Education (7)
8. Healthcare (2, plus tagged templates from other categories)
9. Real Estate (5)
10. Community & Membership (4)
11. Internal Requests & Inspections (7)
12. Consent & Legal (3)
13. Fun & Polls (5, Wave 3)

A template has one main category and optional tags, so it can show in more than one place (for example Schedule a Viewing: Bookings and Real Estate; Liability Waiver: Consent and Healthcare).
